Why us

Why what we build is different

Most tech companies build tools that watch you and sell access to what they learn. Deliravi is built the other way around: the company's role is to understand you deeply — and to keep that understanding yours alone.

Deliravi Typical platforms Chatbots
Understands who you are Deep psychological portrait Click history & engagement metrics Weak, session-level personalization
Your data belongs to you E2EE, your key, never sold Harvested and monetized Partial
Built for self-overcoming Serves your goals, any domain Optimized for watch time General-purpose only

None of the studied solutions — chatbots like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Lumo, or taste apps like TasteRay, Taste, Goodreads and Swipeify — combine all three at once. And copying the code isn't enough: the advantage lives in the accumulated, specially structured data and the learning process built on it. That accumulation is the company we are building.
